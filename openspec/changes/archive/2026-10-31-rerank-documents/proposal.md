Refs #402. Builds on the `reranker` capability (#367, archived 2026-10-30) and on heading-aware chunks (#355) with the chunk minimum (#390, `CHUNKING_VERSION` 2). Scope: **which documents the reranker is sent, and which candidates they come from**. The fusion offset and snippet order in `src/services/hybrid.py` belong to `hybrid-any-fill` (#403) and are not restated here; this change only adds the per-chunk candidate text to the hybrid rerank path.

## Why

With `rerank=true` the reranker demotes notes that both arms ranked first. In #402, *Virtue by Circumstance* dropped from rank 1 in both arms to outside the top 6, and the RRF #1 for "free will and determinism" fell to #4. The endpoint works: bge-reranker-v2-m3 scores a sanity pair +7.8 against −11. The fault is in the text the server sends, and it was measured live after the version-2 drain (issue #402, last comment):

1. **The reranker never sees the heading.** #355 keeps heading lines out of `chunk_text` and puts the heading path and tags in `embed_prefix`. The embedder sees that prefix. The reranker does not, because `build_document` sends `title + "\n\n" + chunk_text`. The chunk under "Victimhood Olympics" never shows the word *victimhood* to the cross-encoder. Adding the prefix raises every replayed score by 1–2 points.
2. **One chunk per note, and it is the wrong one.** The reranker scores only the chunk the semantic arm chose by cosine distance, after dedup. For this note that chunk (6, §11) is the cross-encoder's weakest at −8.87. Its best is chunk 10 at −0.62 (+0.33 with the prefix). Taking the per-note maximum keeps the note on top.

This has to land before anyone is told to turn `rerank=true` on. As things stand, the opt-in is worse than leaving it off for exactly the queries where both arms already agree.

## What Changes

- **The rerank document is the embedding input** (owner decision 1): `embed_prefix + "\n\n" + fmt[embedded_chunking_version](chunk_text)`, cut to `RERANK_DOC_CHARS` (2,048) at a code-point boundary. This is the text the embedder scored, so the cross-encoder sees the heading path and tags. The input is rebuilt from **stored columns** (`embed_prefix`, `chunk_text`, the note's stamp) and never re-derived from the vault at query time (owner decision 7). Three cases keep today's document, `title + "\n\n" + text`: a stale note (semantic: its title alone; hybrid: its title and `body_excerpt`), a keyword-only hybrid candidate (title and excerpt), and a pre-034 chunk row with a NULL prefix (title and chunk). A fresh note whose `embedded_chunking_version` is not a key of `EMBEDDED_TEXT_FORMATTERS` (a later version after a rollback) is sent as its title alone, because its vector's input cannot be reconstructed.
- **Candidates are reranked per chunk, scores are taken per note** (owner decision 2, revised after Codex round 1). The candidate set is **exactly the incumbent's**. Every candidate note first receives its primary document (its cosine-best chunk, or its single title/excerpt document), so no note the incumbent scored is left unscored. The candidates' other already-fetched chunk rows are then allocated in **global distance order** (ties broken by `(file_path, chunk_index)`), with at most **4 documents per note** (`RERANK_MAX_DOCUMENTS_PER_NOTE`, a constant) and at most **`RERANK_MAX_DOCUMENTS` per call** (a new setting, default 100, range 50–200). Documents ≤ min(budget, 4 × candidates). For hybrid's 50 candidates that is 50 primaries plus ≤ 50 secondaries. There is no extra retrieval, no extra statement and no extra round trip. A note's `rerank_score` is the **maximum** over its allocated documents. Notes are ordered by that maximum, then by unreranked position. Whether the #402 note's winning chunk is actually allocated for the live queries is **measured** (a read-only replay task), not claimed.
- **The preview and locator follow the score.** On a successfully reranked row whose winning document is a chunk, **both** the preview and the `§` locator come from that chunk: on `semantic_search` when it is not the primary (a primary winner is already the incumbent's preview), and on `hybrid_search` for every chunk winner, the primary included, even over the keyword snippet. When that chunk has a recorded section, `read_note(section="#N")` opens the section the reranker scored; a pre-034 winner shows no locator (design D4). A title or excerpt winner, and every fallback page, keep the incumbent preview selection. The `similarity` field keeps its value.
- **Oracle** (owner decision 5). `rerank=false` pages stay byte-identical. A `rerank=true` page changes only in row order (and with it which candidates fill the page), in `rerank_score` values, and in the preview and locator of rows whose winning chunk differs from the incumbent preview: a non-primary chunk on either tool, and explicitly a hybrid primary chunk that replaces a keyword snippet. `candidates: N` is unchanged. Fallback pages equal today's baseline.
- **Unchanged** (owner decision 6): the all-or-nothing contract, the closed reason codes (now judged over the flattened document list), the probe, the re-probe, `/health rerank`, the tool schemas and the header and row grammar. This is not a tool-contract change.
- **Latency is documented from measurement** (owner decision 3). On a P4000, uncontended, 50 documents of about 410 characters take 1.6 s, and 50 of about 2,000 characters take 7.3 s. The code default `RERANK_TIMEOUT_MS=1500` stays. The setting's range becomes **100–30000** ms (owner decision, 2026-10-31), because today's `le=10000` would refuse the 20 s that the reference deployment will set in infra (not in this repo). **The budget and the timeout are read together:** 100 documents of ~410 characters take about 3.2 s, and 100 of ~2 KB about 15 s, uncontended (scaled from the measurement). The 20 s setting is **contingent on the concurrency mode**: it is allowed in `off`/`shadow`. Under `queue`/`enforce`, DEPLOYMENT.md requires `RERANK_TIMEOUT_MS ≤ MCP_CONCURRENCY_WAIT_SECONDS` unless `make concurrency-report` with rerank traffic passes, because one `rerank=true` call holds the sole embedding slot, and concurrent `semantic_search`/`hybrid_search` calls are refused with `slot_timeout` under `enforce` (accepted limitation L10).
- **Telemetry**: a `rerank=true` usage row adds `rerank_documents`, the number of documents sent.
- **Gate depths**: the gate runs the candidate and incumbent arms at call limits 10 and 20 (`rerank-l10`, `rerank-l20`; recall@10/@20 and ndcg@10 are the cells there, and recall@50 is null by `staged_depth`) as well as at depth 50. A depth-50 report alone cannot see crowding or max bias at the limits agents use.
- **Harness**: `make_semantic_arm_incumbent` and `make_hybrid_arm_incumbent` are frozen copies of today's builder and candidate set. They are the `BASELINE` arms for the eval gate. The issue's two queries are named judgments, reported **for information only**, as in #403.
- **Declined** (owner decision 4): a weak-score skip (`rerank: skipped (weak scores)`). It is a heuristic with no evidence behind it. It is recorded as a follow-up, together with the evidence that would justify it (design D9).
- Docs in the same PR: `docs/architecture/search.md` ("Reranking (#367)": the scored unit, the budget, the max rule, the locator, L2 retired), `docs/architecture/indexing-and-embeddings.md` (the embedding input now has a second reader), the CLAUDE.md reranker bullet, DEPLOYMENT.md (the latency table and the infra note), README (one sentence, plus the timeout range in the settings table), and `.env.example` (the range).

No migration. One new setting (`RERANK_MAX_DOCUMENTS`). No change to the probe, the wire format or the client.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `reranker`: MODIFIED "The reranker SHALL be configured by four optional settings…" (the `RERANK_TIMEOUT_MS` range becomes 100–30000; DEPLOYMENT latency, budget and concurrency rules), ADDED "RERANK_MAX_DOCUMENTS SHALL bound the documents one rerank call sends", "A rerank=true call SHALL score only the candidates the search already fetched…" (the document is the embedding input, every candidate's primary first and secondaries in distance order under the budget and the cap of 4, the score is the per-note max, the preview and locator follow it, and the projection gains two columns under `rerank=true` only), "rerank on hybrid_search SHALL rerank the first 50 fused rows…" (the same allocation over fused order), "Any rerank failure SHALL return the unreranked page…" (validation over the document list; the fallback keeps the cosine-best preview) and "The usage row SHALL record whether a call was reranked…" (`rerank_documents`).
- `search-quality`: MODIFIED "Search calls record per-phase timing" (it lists the rerank usage keys and gains `rerank_documents`), and MODIFIED "Each fresh vector-search row SHALL name the section it matched…". That requirement forbids projecting `embed_prefix`; the change carves out the `rerank=true` overfetch statement, which may project exactly `embed_prefix` and `embedded_chunking_version`, while `rerank=false` and `find_related` keep the prohibition.
- `search-evaluation`: MODIFIED "The harness SHALL ship replayable rerank arms…" (the two incumbent arms, and the arms rerank through the shared allocation).

## Impact

- `src/services/rerank.py`: `build_document` gains the embedding-input form, `allocate_documents` and `RERANK_MAX_DOCUMENTS_PER_NOTE = 4` are new, and `rerank_candidates` takes per-note document groups and returns the per-note max plus the winning document. The exchange, validation, probe and suppressor are unchanged.
- `src/services/embeddings.py` (`semantic_arm_rows` with `with_rerank_text`, `semantic_candidates`): under `rerank=true` only, the projection adds `NoteEmbedding.embed_prefix` and `NoteMetadata.embedded_chunking_version`, and each deduped row carries its note's other fetched chunks. With `rerank=false` the statement is unchanged.
- `src/services/hybrid.py`: the semantic-arm chunk groups are carried onto `HybridRow` when `rerank_candidates`. `_fill_excerpts` is unchanged.
- `src/mcp_server/tools.py`: `_semantic_search_reranked` and `_hybrid_search_reranked` allocate, rerank, and swap a row's chunk and section to the winning chunk. `_record_rerank` adds `rerank_documents`.
- `src/config.py`: the `rerank_timeout_ms` ceiling and `rerank_max_documents`.
- `scripts/search_eval_arms/rerank.py`: the incumbent factories. A read-only measurement script for the two #402 queries (numbers only, no tenant text).
- Tests: a synthetic fixture in which the winning chunk is allocated by construction, the allocation bound proof test, byte-identity of `rerank=false`, the `rerank=true` oracle, locator parity, and updates to the existing rerank tests whose one-document-per-note assumptions change.
- Gates: the eval gate (the `gate` step from `hybrid-any-fill`, 8 cells, CI95 lower bound ≥ −0.01, incumbent arm as `BASELINE`), `openspec-verifier`, and an adversarial Codex pass, which is mandatory because an agent acts on this search path. **Sequencing: this change merges after `hybrid-any-fill` (#403)**, because it uses that change's `gate` step and its harness incumbent-arm pattern.
