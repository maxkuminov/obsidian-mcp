## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: The response SHALL be bounded by the read-response character cap at every setting, measured on rendered (escaped) length, dropping whole rows and always rendering a bounded first row
`hybrid_search`'s response SHALL be at most `MAX_READ_RESPONSE_CHARS` characters (minimum 1,000), for every value of `HYBRID_RRF_K` and on both the both-arms and the keyword-only path. Every length in this requirement is a **rendered** length: characters after `change_feed.quote` escaping, in which a control character costs six (`\u00XX`) and `"` or `\` costs two. A cut never splits an escape sequence and never adds a character inside a literal; the cut is stated only by a server label after the separator or in the header. The query echo SHALL be the longest prefix of whole characters of the query whose rendered literal content is at most 64 characters. When that is not the whole query, the header SHALL carry `query_cut`. The header SHALL be at most 400 characters in its worst case: the 66-character query literal, `query_cut`, `rrf_k: 1000`, the keyword-only token with the longer code, and every count, each at most 100 by construction (arms ≤ 50, union ≤ 100, snippet cap ≤ 100). The keyword-only footer SHALL be one fixed sentence of at most 75 characters, and the response-cap footer SHALL be at most 75 characters. Rows SHALL be appended in fused order while each fits after the header and footers are reserved. The first row that does not fit SHALL end the page. The header's `shown` SHALL count the rendered rows, and the footer SHALL state how many results were omitted. Later rows SHALL be dropped whole and never cut. When the first row does not fit, it SHALL be abbreviated in order, stopping as soon as it fits: drop the tags (`tags omitted`); drop the preview (`preview omitted`); then cut the title (`title_cut`) and the path (`path_cut`). Each cut keeps the shortest prefix of whole characters whose rendered content is at least 40 characters, so the kept content is 40 to 45 rendered characters, which may be as few as 7 raw characters of a control-heavy value. A value whose whole rendered content is at most 45 characters is not cut. If even that does not fit, the field SHALL render as the omitted form `"title":""` / `"path":""` with `title_cut` / `path_cut` and `title omitted` / `path omitted`. At the 1,000-character floor this last form is unreachable by the arithmetic below. Every label SHALL be server-authored and placed after the separator. The row SHALL be marked `abbreviated`. The abbreviated row's structure SHALL be at most 345 characters (340 before #391; the longest `matched_in` value grew from `title+body` to `title+tags+body`, five characters). The structure is `- `, the fixed JSON keys and quotes with empty values, the separator, every server field at its worst case (`k = 1000`, ranks up to 50, `matched_in: title+tags+body`, both markers) and every label of the cut form (`tags omitted`, `preview omitted`, `title_cut`, `path_cut`, `abbreviated`). The omitted form is always shorter than the cut form: it removes at least 80 rendered characters of title and path content and adds the 31 characters of `title omitted` and `path omitted`. The worst case at the floor SHALL be header 400 + row structure 345 + title 45 + path 45 + keyword-only footer 75 + cap footer 75 + at most 5 line breaks = 990 ≤ 1,000, so the 40–45 character cut form stays guaranteed at `rerank=false`.

#### Scenario: Worst case at the minimum cap, both arms, control-heavy
- **WHEN** `MAX_READ_RESPONSE_CHARS=1000` and `HYBRID_RRF_K=1000`; the query is 8,192 control characters; and the first result, found by both arms, has a 512-character title of control characters, a 1,024-character path holding control characters, `"` and `\`, 1,000 tags and a preview
- **THEN** the response SHALL be at most 1,000 rendered characters; the header SHALL carry a query literal of at most 64 rendered content characters and `query_cut`; and the response SHALL contain one abbreviated row whose title and path literals each hold 40–45 rendered characters with no split escape, with `title_cut`, `path_cut`, `tags omitted`, `preview omitted` and `abbreviated`

#### Scenario: Worst case at the minimum cap, keyword-only, control-heavy
- **WHEN** the same inputs are searched while the provider raises `ProviderInputTooLarge`
- **THEN** the response SHALL be at most 1,000 rendered characters, and SHALL carry `query_cut`, `arms: keyword-only (semantic unavailable: provider_input_rejected)`, the fixed keyword-only footer, and the abbreviated row with 40–45 rendered characters each of title and path

#### Scenario: The cut never splits an escape
- **WHEN** a title consists of `"` and U+0001 alternating, and the row is abbreviated
- **THEN** the kept title literal SHALL parse as a JSON string, its content SHALL be 40–45 rendered characters, and it SHALL decode to a prefix of the title

#### Scenario: The omitted form when the kept prefix cannot fit
- **WHEN** the test first measures the rendered length `L_omit` of the response whose first row is in the omitted form and the length `L_cut` of the same response in the cut form, asserts `L_cut > L_omit`, and then calls the renderer directly with the budget `L_omit`
- **THEN** it SHALL render `"title":""` and `"path":""` with `title_cut`, `title omitted`, `path_cut` and `path omitted`, and the response SHALL be at most `L_omit` characters

#### Scenario: The longest matched_in value is inside the pinned structure bound

- **WHEN** the abbreviated first row's structure is rendered at its worst case with `matched_in: title+tags+body`, `HYBRID_RRF_K=1000`, ranks of 50 and both markers, on a `rerank=false` page
- **THEN** its length SHALL be at most 345 characters, the pinned constant SHALL be 345, and the worst-case control-heavy response at `MAX_READ_RESPONSE_CHARS=1000` SHALL keep title and path literals of 40–45 rendered characters each
