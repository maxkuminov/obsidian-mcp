{# The Tasks settings card (#428, design D14), included by `account.html` (multi-user mode: the signed-in user's own row) and `settings.html` (single-user mode: the NULL-scope row). Context: `tasks_card`, built by `routes._task_settings_context`; the form posts to `tasks_card.action`. CSP (#195, #289): no inline handler, no `style=`, no `javascript:` URL, no script — every control is a plain form field, so nothing here needs `panel.js`. Classes are the panel's existing components and generated utilities only. The four write behaviours are three-way selects, not checkboxes: "server default" (stored NULL, follows the environment) must stay expressible, and `task_settings.save` reads the literal "default" as exactly that. #}
