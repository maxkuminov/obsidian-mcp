{#- The panel's single token source. Included from the of `base.html`, `auth_base.html` and `authorize.html` — the three roots of the panel/auth surface. None of them keeps a palette of its own, so they cannot drift apart again (they had: auth_base carried a duplicate copy of the token block and authorize.html an entirely separate set of literals, which is why every `--consent-*` token below exists). Dark is canonical. Every value in the bare `:root` block is byte-identical to the literal it replaced in the pre-token templates — see `openspec/changes/panel-light-mode/baseline-manifest.json` and the replay check that proves it. Light is the override set. Theme selection: bare `:root` → dark (the tested baseline) `:root[data-theme="light"]` → explicit light `prefers-color-scheme: light`, guarded `:root:not([data-theme="dark"])` → OS default The transfer pages (`transfer_upload.html`, `transfer_download.html`) are a DIFFERENT surface and deliberately do not include this partial: they serve third parties under a strict per-request nonce CSP with static-response discipline, so they carry their own local token block, stay OS-responsive, and have no toggle and no localStorage. -#}
